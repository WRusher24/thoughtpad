# ThoughtPad — User Guide

Welcome to **ThoughtPad**, your quiet corner of the internet: a warm, paper-like
notepad built for one thing — getting thoughts out of your head *fast*.

---

## 1. Getting in

- **New here?** Tap **Create your notepad**, pick a username and a password
  (8+ characters), and you are in — instantly, no email required.
- **Returning?** Log in with your username and password.
- Your notepad is **completely private**. Nobody else can ever see your
  thoughts, drawers, archive, or trash — every account is its own sealed
  notebook.

> Trying the demo? Log in with **demo / thoughtpad123** to explore a
> pre-filled notepad.

---

## 2. The instant typing box

The moment you arrive, the cursor is already blinking in a clean, lined
text area. No menus, no setup — just type.

- The box greets you with **rotating prompts** ("Dump it before you
  forget…") until you start typing.
- Tap the solid black **Save** button (bottom-left of the box) — or press
  **Ctrl/Cmd + Enter** — and the thought lands in your stream immediately.
  The box clears itself, ready for the next dump. No reload, no waiting.
- Write in **any language** — English, Arabic, anything. Mixed-language
  dumps are welcome; each card automatically aligns its own text.

### Smart filing ("Brain Drawers")

You never have to organize anything. When you save a thought, ThoughtPad
reads it and files it into the best-fitting drawer:

- `buy eggs and oat milk` → **Errands**
- `email Priya the projections before Thursday` → **Work** (and because it
  sounds actionable, it becomes a **task** you can check off)
- `app idea: focus timer that grows a zen garden` → **Ideas**

Anything it can't place goes to your **Inbox**. The sorter quietly learns
from your own captures, so it keeps getting better the more you write.

---

## 3. Your stream

Below the box, thoughts stack **newest-first** as clean paper cards.

- **Check off a task** — tap the circle on the left of a task card. It
  slides straight into the **Archive**. (Non-task cards have a small
  archive icon, top-right, for the same move.)
- **Edit** — tap the tiny **pencil** on any card. Rewrite the text, and
  either let ThoughtPad **Auto-sort** it again or pick a drawer yourself
  from the chips. **Ctrl/Cmd + Enter** saves.
- **Delete** — the little trash icon sends a card to **Recently Deleted**
  (it asks once, gently, before moving).
- **Search** — tap the **magnifying glass** (top-right). The header melts
  into a search bar and filters as you type — keywords *or* drawer names.

## 4. The sidebar (hamburger, top-left)

| Section             | What lives there                                                              |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stream**          | Your live, unarchived thoughts.                                                |
| **Archive**         | Checked-off and filed-away thoughts — unarchive or trash any of them.          |
| **Brain Drawers**   | Create, restyle, or remove your custom categories (see below).                 |
| **Recently Deleted**| A safety net: restore anything, or wipe it forever ("Empty trash" included).   |
| **Settings**        | Fonts, data export, account.                                                   |
| **Filter by drawer**| Tap any drawer in the list to see only its thoughts in the stream.             |

### Creating your own drawers

Open **Brain Drawers → New**. Give it a name, a paper-toned color, and an
icon. From that moment, the sorter knows about it — say you create
**Gift Ideas**, then save *"gift for mom: that silk scarf from the souk"*;
it lands there automatically. Deleting a drawer never deletes thoughts —
they are re-filed into your Inbox.

---

## 5. Settings

- **Font style** — three moods for your notepad, applied everywhere:
  - **Classic Clean** — crisp modern sans.
  - **Playful Serif** — Fraunces headlines, easy-reading body (default).
  - **Typewriter Mono** — full Courier Prime letterpress vibe.
- **Export your data** — one tap downloads everything you own:
  **JSON** (complete, structured) or **CSV** (spreadsheet-ready).
- **Account** — your stats at a glance and sign-out.

## 6. Install it like a real app

ThoughtPad is a full PWA. On your phone, open the browser menu and choose
**Add to Home Screen**. It installs with its own parchment icon and
launches **full-screen**, hiding the browser bars — a pocket notepad,
always one tap away.

---

### A thirty-second ritual

> Open → type → Save → walk away.
> Check tasks off when done. Search when you need. Export whenever.
> Your mind stays clear; your thoughts stay safe.
